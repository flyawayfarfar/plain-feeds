# Plain Feeds privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

**Plain Feeds collects no personal data.**

- **What is stored:** the feeds you add, the articles they contain and which ones you have read. All of it is stored only in your browser's extension storage, on your device. Removing the extension deletes it.
- **What is sent:** Plain Feeds downloads the feeds you add from the sites that publish them, without cookies or login details. It sends nothing to the developer or any other party, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
- **Permissions:**
  - *Storage and unlimited storage:* save your feeds and read state on your device.
  - *Alarms:* check your feeds every 30 minutes.
  - *Active tab and scripting:* when you click the toolbar icon, look for feed links on the page you are viewing. Nothing on the page is stored or sent.
  - *Offscreen document (Chrome and Edge):* read feed files, because the background part of an extension cannot do it directly.
  - *Site access:* requested separately for each site whose feed you add, and used only to download that feed.
- **Third parties:** none. When you open an article, it opens on the publisher's own website under that website's own privacy policy.
- **Changes:** any change to this policy will be published here with a new date. If a paid upgrade is ever added, its payment provider and what it receives will be listed here before release.
- **Contact:** open an issue in this repository.
